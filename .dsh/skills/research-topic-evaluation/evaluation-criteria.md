# Lightweight evaluation criteria

Use `低 / 中 / 高 / 暂无法判断`. Give one or two evidence sentences per dimension. Do not calculate an opaque total score.

## Research heat

Compare exact-topic and parent-technology research-paper counts, plus their first and second 12-month counts inside the two-year window.

## Academic impact

Use the journal distribution, verified Nature Index journal membership, and up to five representative research results. Do not use CAS partition statistics in Module 01.

## Research maturity

Judge whether the exact-topic research shows repeated independent demonstrations, recognizable technical routes, comparable metrics, and movement beyond isolated proofs of concept.

## Review-writing value

Judge whether the exact-topic corpus is sufficient for a focused review and whether the parent-technology corpus supplies a useful technical frame. This is a preliminary value judgment; Module 01 does not search or count existing reviews.

## Recommendation

- `建议继续`: enough exact-topic research and a coherent parent field support review writing.
- `建议调整题目后继续`: the exact topic is too narrow or broad, but a nearby framing is viable.
- `暂缓决定`: retrieval or journal verification is too incomplete.
- `不建议继续`: sufficient evidence shows too little coherent research material.

Only the user can approve continuation.
