# Module 03 outline rules

## Structural requirements

- Use decimal numbering such as `1`, `2`, `2.1`, `2.1.1`.
- Store sections in numeric reading order.
- Use 3–10 top-level sections and no more than four levels.
- Number siblings consecutively and include every parent section.
- Include a top-level Introduction and a top-level Future Perspective or Outlook section.
- The selected Module 02 perspective determines the primary organizing axis. Do not revert to a generic materials-devices-applications catalogue unless that is the selected perspective.

## Required content for every section

Every section, including subsections, must provide:

- purpose;
- ordered writing logic;
- key questions;
- reason for its position and inclusion;
- persisted Module 02 evidence ids when available;
- expected reader takeaway;
- transition from or to neighboring sections.

A heading list alone is invalid.

## Evidence and scope

- Use only evidence already persisted by Module 02.
- An Introduction or synthesis section may have no specific evidence id, but must explain its role.
- Evidence ids are internal audit links only. User-facing outlines must resolve them into the specific review title and motivation/trends, research-paper title and classified finding, or TrendClaim label and description.
- Never display raw identifiers such as `W4412186979`, `review_...`, or `trend_...` as the evidence explanation.
- Missing evidence must be stated as a limitation, not filled with invented references.
- Adjacent-review evidence must remain labeled as adjacent and cannot be described as an exact-topic review.

## Iteration

- Each revision creates a new immutable version.
- Preserve history and the exact user feedback that requested revision.
- Do not silently broaden the scope while revising.
- Approval freezes the latest version only.

## Prohibited outputs

Module 03 must not generate manuscript prose, an abstract, an introduction draft, section paragraphs, reference lists formatted for submission, figures, Word, or LaTeX.
